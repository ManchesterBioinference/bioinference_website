I am a Research Assistant in the Division of Informatics, Imaging and Data Sciences at the University of Manchester, with an academic background in Biochemistry and an MSc in Health Data Science. I am particularly interested in developing interpretable AI frameworks to investigate transcriptional regulation, with applications in developmental biology and human blood cell formation.

My broader research interests include computational biology, single-cell multi-omics, cancer biology and the application of artificial intelligence to biomedical research. I am passionate about bridging experimental biology and computational approaches to generate meaningful biological insights and advance translational research.
